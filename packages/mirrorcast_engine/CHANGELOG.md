## 1.0.0

* First release of the MirrorCast native engine.
* Android: MediaProjection capture, hardware H.264/H.265 encoding, AudioPlaybackCapture,
  TLS 1.3 client with Keystore identity and fingerprint pinning, PIN and QR pairing.
* macOS: TLS 1.3 server (Network.framework) with keychain identity, VideoToolbox decoding
  into a zero-copy Flutter texture, low-latency AVAudioEngine playback, window modes.
